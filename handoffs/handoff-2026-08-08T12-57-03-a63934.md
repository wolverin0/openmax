## Context

FuturaMAX is an R&D project to determine how much additional real-world PtMP capacity, spectral efficiency, latency stability, fairness, and operational intelligence can be extracted from existing Ubiquiti airMAX AC hardware. The user asked this pane to review a long ChatGPT Deep Research conversation, inspect its generated project guides, design a first-pass multi-model web-research prompt, evaluate `Weizhena/Deep-Research-skills`, and leave a durable handoff for another pane to orchestrate all incoming findings into trustworthy project knowledge.

The target hardware family currently includes LiteAP GPS/LAP-GPS APs, LiteBeam 5AC Gen2 CPEs, NanoStation 5AC CPEs, and Loco 5AC CPEs. QCA988x/QCA9882 and AR9342-class components are research hypotheses until verified per exact model and revision. LTU is outside the primary research scope.

The central engineering question is:

> What is the best fixed-wireless system the existing AC hardware can physically execute when optimized for stationary outdoor PtMP links, known topology and distances, known service plans, historical per-CPE behavior, hidden nodes, real WISP interference, and control of both AP and CPE where technically possible?

The project is not attempting to convert 802.11ac into Wi-Fi 6/7, manufacture unsupported PHY features, or prove in advance that FuturaMAX can beat Ubiquiti. A valid outcome may retain airMAX TDMA and add better external telemetry, queueing, pacing, spectrum intelligence, per-CPE models, and network-wide optimization.

## Operating Objective

The receiving pane must build an evidence system, not merely a long narrative report. Preserve raw model outputs, extract their claims and sources, verify important claims against primary sources, record contradictions, and publish a canonical knowledge layer that future engineering agents can trust.

The evidence pipeline must answer:

1. What can be improved without replacing airOS?
2. What requires OpenWrt or custom host firmware?
3. What requires QCA988x firmware changes?
4. What requires coordinated AP and CPE changes?
5. What is controlled by proprietary airMAX components?
6. What is probably blocked or still unknown?
7. Which reported improvements were measured on real hardware under relevant conditions?
8. Which techniques have a credible path to the exact target hardware revisions?
9. Which experiments have the highest information value?
10. What evidence would show that stock airMAX is already superior?

## Research Pipeline

### R0 - Intake readiness and provenance

Goal: establish where every input came from before agents start interpreting it.

Required actions:

- Inspect the repository without assuming it is initialized or clean.
- Query MemoryMaster for `FuturaMAX` decisions and constraints.
- Create the proposed directory structure only when beginning orchestration.
- Assign a stable `run_id` to every external Deep Research run.
- Record provider, model, prompt, start date, completion date, supplied files, and source-link count.
- Preserve every raw report byte-for-byte; never rewrite or “clean up” the only copy.
- Hash raw files so later transformations have traceable provenance.

Exit gate:

- Every supplied report is represented in `research/intake/manifest.yaml`.
- Raw originals exist under `research/raw/<provider>/<run_id>/`.
- No findings have yet been promoted into canonical knowledge without traceability.

### R1 - Independent broad web discovery

Goal: collect independent research from ChatGPT Deep Research, Qwen, Gemini, and any other web-capable model chosen by the user.

Rules:

- Give each model the same FuturaMAX web-research prompt.
- Do not show one model another model's result during this stage.
- Require direct source URLs and exact measured results when available.
- Search older foundational work and current follow-up work through the present.
- Require supporting and negative evidence.
- Save complete reports, citations, generated tables, downloadable files, and the exact prompt.
- A model's polished prose is not evidence; the linked source is the evidence candidate.

Exit gate:

- At least two independent model runs have been ingested, or the manifest explicitly records why only one exists.
- Each run retains its source list and prompt.

### R2 - Mechanical normalization

Goal: convert heterogeneous reports into machine-readable records without judging truth yet.

Produce append-only JSONL datasets:

- `research/normalized/sources.jsonl`
- `research/normalized/claims.jsonl`
- `research/normalized/measurements.jsonl`
- `research/normalized/candidates.jsonl`
- `research/normalized/conflicts.jsonl`

Normalization work includes:

- Canonicalize URLs and DOI identifiers.
- Merge obvious duplicate source records while preserving every referring run.
- Split compound prose into atomic claims.
- Extract exact quantitative measurements separately from general claims.
- Preserve model uncertainty and wording; do not silently strengthen it.
- Mark missing fields instead of fabricating them.

Exit gate:

- Every material report claim has a source reference, `source_missing`, or `model_only` marker.
- Every percentage improvement is tied to a baseline and conditions, or explicitly marked incomplete.

### R3 - Source verification and citation hardening

Goal: determine what each cited source actually establishes.

Source priority:

1. Measurements on the exact target device or chipset.
2. Source code and commits for the exact implementation.
3. Official Linux, OpenWrt, vendor, regulatory, FCC, GPL, or patent material.
4. Peer-reviewed real-hardware research.
5. Strong preprints with code, data, and reproducible methods.
6. Maintainer mailing-list discussions and issue threads.
7. Simulations, SDR prototypes, community reports, blogs, and forums.

Required checks:

- Open the direct source, not a search-result page.
- Verify title, authors or organization, publication date, and stable locator.
- Confirm the cited measurement appears in the source.
- Record exact hardware, firmware, topology, station count, RF conditions, traffic, baseline, treatment, metric, repetitions, and statistics.
- Search for code, datasets, follow-up papers, forward citations, replications, corrections, and contradictory results.
- Record whether the source is current, superseded, abandoned, or still maintained.
- Do not quote beyond copyright limits; store short evidence snippets and precise locators.

Exit gate:

- All Tier-S candidate techniques and all claims used in the executive synthesis have at least one verified primary source or are explicitly downgraded.

### R4 - Targeted gap research

Goal: use agents to fill missing evidence after broad discovery, not repeat the same general survey.

Use a project-adapted version of `Weizhena/Deep-Research-skills` if helpful. Do not install or modify global Codex configuration without explicit user approval. If vendored project-locally, change its generic schema and behavior as follows:

- Preserve uncertain values in reports; never drop them.
- Add FuturaMAX evidence fields defined later in this handoff.
- Prefer primary technical sources over community summaries.
- Validate source accessibility and field semantics, not only JSON field coverage.
- Remove or update hardcoded model choices based on the active environment.
- Treat research items as mechanisms, hardware boundaries, or explicit gaps rather than generic products.
- Resume completed items without overwriting prior results.

Each targeted agent receives one bounded question, known sources, missing fields, and a required output path. Agents must report failed searches and negative evidence.

Exit gate:

- The gap ledger distinguishes `filled`, `partially_filled`, `not_found`, and `requires_physical_experiment`.

### R5 - Workstream synthesis

Goal: build specialist research packets without prematurely merging different control layers.

Recommended workstreams:

- `W01` exact hardware, board revisions, partitions, device trees, GPS, and possible accelerator hardware.
- `W02` airOS/airMAX architecture, proprietary TDMA, GPS sync, patents, GPL and FCC evidence.
- `W03` Linux, mac80211, cfg80211, ath10k, ath10k-ct, WMI, HTT, queues, descriptors, and observability.
- `W04` TDMA, polling, hMAC, Det-WiFi, WiLDNet, SoftMAC, OpenFWWF, MadWifi, and FreeBSD scheduled MAC work.
- `W05` rate adaptation, MCS/NSS choice, rate masks, retry chains, Minstrel, EDRA, IteRate, and learned controllers.
- `W06` A-MPDU/A-MSDU, PNOFA, aggregate duration, Block ACK, retransmissions, and pacing effects.
- `W07` TXQ, AQL, airtime scheduling, FQ-CoDel, CAKE, subscriber pacing, fairness, and bufferbloat.
- `W08` hidden nodes, RTS/CTS, near/far capture, contention, CCA, ANI, and spatial reuse.
- `W09` QCA988x FFT, interference classification, spectrum history, conflict graphs, and channel/width/power optimization.
- `W10` long-distance timing, coverage class, ACK/CTS timing, GPS alignment, propagation delay, and outdoor behavior.
- `W11` chainmask, NSS, SGI, STBC, LDPC, beamforming where supported, TX power, chain imbalance, and calibration constraints.
- `W12` automated experimentation, RF testbeds, statistical gates, replay, digital twins, regression detection, and rollback.
- `W13` recent papers, patches, releases, patents, abandoned projects, and maintenance status.
- `W14` red-team/refutation: reasons a well-tuned airMAX baseline may remain superior.

Batch workstreams according to available concurrency. Do not spawn all workstreams at once. Respect the active agent system's subagent limits and use one owner per output file.

Exit gate:

- Every workstream packet includes positive evidence, negative evidence, control-boundary ownership, portability analysis, gaps, and proposed experiments.

### R6 - Contradiction and portability audit

Goal: prevent results from adjacent platforms from becoming false QCA988x claims.

For each important claim, classify the evidence:

- `A`: exact target hardware or exact chipset with real measurements.
- `B`: comparable 802.11ac hardware with a credible porting path.
- `C`: adjacent chipset or older Atheros hardware; concept may transfer, implementation uncertain.
- `D`: simulation, SDR, or materially different PHY/MAC.
- `E`: unverified community or vendor claim.

Also classify the claim status:

- `CONFIRMED`
- `DEMONSTRATED_ADJACENT`
- `INFERRED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `REFUTED`

Every technique must identify its likely owner:

- `EXTERNAL_CONTROLLER`
- `LINUX_NETWORKING`
- `MAC80211`
- `ATH10K_DRIVER`
- `QCA988X_FIRMWARE`
- `RADIO_HARDWARE`
- `PROPRIETARY_AIRMAX`
- `REQUIRES_AP_AND_CPE`
- `UNKNOWN`

The red-team agent must actively search for OpenWrt regressions, hidden-node collapse, firmware-owned controls, deep hidden queues, inaccurate telemetry, rate-mask instability, missing aggregation hooks, CPU/RAM/flash limits, abandoned implementations, and failures to reproduce published gains.

Exit gate:

- No `CONFIRMED` claim relies solely on adjacent hardware.
- Contradictory sources remain visible and are not averaged into a false consensus.
- At least 25 percent of the synthesis discusses limitations, negative results, or uncertainty.

### R7 - Canonical knowledge compilation

Goal: publish concise, source-backed knowledge files that future agents can navigate quickly.

Canonical files are listed under `Proposed Repository Structure`. Each substantially edited document must begin with a dense seven-line header stating coverage, keywords, current verdict, and when to read it. `docs/DOCS-MAP.md` must classify every document as `CURRENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `ABANDONED`, or `GENERATED`.

Rules:

- Knowledge files summarize verified evidence; raw reports remain elsewhere.
- Generated indexes never override current source evidence.
- Every material conclusion links to source IDs from `sources.jsonl`.
- Keep an opportunity ledger separate from accepted recommendations.
- Preserve `UNKNOWN` rather than omitting it.
- Time-sensitive statements include an `accessed_at` or `verified_at` date.

Exit gate:

- A fresh agent can start at `docs/DOCS-MAP.md`, then `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md`, then `knowledge/INDEX.md` without reading raw reports.

### R8 - Experiment mapping

Goal: translate evidence into falsifiable experiments without implementing radio changes.

Every proposed experiment must state:

- experiment ID,
- hypothesis,
- evidence source IDs,
- required control hook,
- exact hardware and revision,
- firmware/build hash requirements,
- topology and RF conditions,
- baseline,
- treatment,
- offered-load trace,
- primary metric,
- secondary metrics,
- repetitions and randomization,
- confidence interval or statistical test,
- acceptance threshold,
- rejection threshold,
- safety and regulatory invariants,
- rollback condition,
- expected raw and processed artifacts,
- `KEEP`, `REJECT`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` decision rule.

No experiment may claim physical success from mocks, simulations, parser tests, or generated reports.

Exit gate:

- The top five low-risk experiments and top five feasibility/kill experiments are fully specified.
- `knowledge/CUSTOM_MAC_GO_NO_GO.md` remains `UNKNOWN` until physical control-boundary tests exist.

### R9 - Research release gate

Goal: decide whether the knowledge base is ready to guide engineering Stage E0.

Required review:

- Source ledger completeness.
- Broken-link and duplicate-source check.
- Claim-to-source traceability.
- Measurement-field completeness.
- Contradiction preservation.
- Hardware-revision specificity.
- Control-boundary completeness.
- Negative-evidence coverage.
- Experiment specification quality.
- Documentation-map currency.

Exit gate:

- Coordinator publishes a research release verdict: `READY_FOR_E0`, `PARTIAL`, or `BLOCKED`.
- The verdict names exact missing evidence and physical user actions.
- A report or a completed JSON directory alone is not proof that the research is ready.

## Engineering Roadmap After Research

These stages describe later project work. They are context for research prioritization, not authorization for the receiving pane to implement them.

### E0 - Research operating system

Build hardware inventory schemas, board-probe tooling, flash-map validation, calibration/factory hashing, protected-partition definitions, reproducible metrics, workload definitions, experiment orchestration, result generation, statistical gates, image-size checks, recovery qualification, and rollback controls.

### E1 - Stock-airOS intelligence

Preserve airMAX. Add read-only per-CPE telemetry/history, POP/core CAKE or FQ-CoDel, subscriber pacing, latency/loss probes, chain-imbalance detection, spectrum history, capacity forecasting, advisory channel/width/power optimization, canary changes, and causal before/after analysis.

### E2 - Isolated OpenWrt observability laboratory

Use spare devices only. Establish reproducible builds, supported initramfs/recovery, upstream ath10k versus ath10k-ct, debugfs/tracepoints, spectral FFT, coverage-class timing, TXQ/AQL, queue visibility, fixed-rate/rate-mask behavior, repeated power-cycle recovery, and return-to-stock proof.

### E3 - Algorithm experiments

Only after control hooks are demonstrated, test adaptive aggregation, per-CPE rate constraints, retry policies, adaptive RTS/CTS, hidden-node classifiers, contained CCA experiments, slow-loop power/channel/width control, and cross-layer controllers.

### E4 - Custom MAC feasibility gate

Before writing TDMA, prove precise TX completion visibility, bounded lower-layer queue ownership, bounded TX release timing, AP/CPE synchronization, controllable ACK/retry interactions, management/beacon survival, safe missed-slot recovery, and usable GPS/timing access if required.

If any essential condition fails, do not force a custom MAC. Redirect effort to the best airOS-preserving or OpenWrt-host-controlled techniques.

### E5 - Custom PtMP MAC

Only if E4 passes, consider centralized polling, demand-based slots, airtime-cost scheduling, contract-aware scheduling, latency deadlines, dynamic DL/UL, GPS-aligned sectors, and propagation-aware timing.

## Constraints & Gotchas

- Research claims from the prior ChatGPT conversation are leads, not current verified facts.
- Hardware-family similarity is not proof of identical board layout, partitions, GPS access, calibration treatment, accelerator hardware, or OpenWrt support.
- Standard ath10k firmware offload may block direct per-frame host rate control, retry-chain control, aggregation control, and deterministic transmit timing; research must locate real hooks before proposing algorithms.
- Replacing airOS with ordinary OpenWrt also removes airMAX scheduling and GPS behavior. Better Linux queueing can coexist with worse hidden-node PtMP capacity.
- The most likely early success is a hybrid system that preserves airMAX and adds external intelligence.
- Published improvements such as aggregation, rate-control, and queueing gains overlap; never sum their percentages.
- `Weizhena/Deep-Research-skills` validates field coverage, not truth. Its default report behavior can hide uncertain values, which is unacceptable here.
- Its installer changes global Codex configuration and its researcher configuration may hardcode a model. Do not install blindly.
- Community sources are useful discovery leads but cannot establish target-hardware control boundaries alone.
- “Current,” “latest,” repository activity, supported-device status, firmware releases, laws, and regulatory rules are time-sensitive and require live verification.
- Physical work remains laboratory-only until recovery is proven.
- Never modify bootloader, ART/calibration, factory data, MAC storage, board data, signatures, DFS behavior, or regulatory limits.
- Never flash production radios.
